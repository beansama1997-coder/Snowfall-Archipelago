A manual implementation of Archipelago for snow•fall. Randomizes characters, stage r functions, rerolling options, and products you can access. Doesn't randomize cube access and you can do the category-free reroll from the start. Win condition is setup as beating node 15 with 3 characters.

Notes:

When you start the archipelago, you'll need to mark the "start the game" check as complete to get your first character. There is no other condition for this check.

You can take all cubes from the start.

Manual archipelagos rely on honor system, but nothing mechanically stops you. You also must manually mark node completion and when you win.

Love words giving you a random product is likely to give a banned item. This is considered allowed as long as love words itself is unlocked.

Rod of compliance lacks a category, while cutter flower is marked "product product." Since this doesn't affect functionality, this will remain the case.

When customizing yaml options, it'll sort snowfall as though starting with "m" due to the name. It broke when I tried to rename the apworld, so it'll just have to do.

I tried to remove the example options but couldn't (sorry), so please ignore them.

While you cannot mechanically change the goal, you could manually, in your head, declare that you win with a different condition if you wish, and only mark the goal once you do so.
